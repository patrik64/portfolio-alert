  group: Fund fetching
  technology: TypeScript, fetch, regular expressions
description: Reads each fund's portfolio page into a list of companies
---

One scraper per tracked fund, plus the index that maps each fund slug to its scraper together with the fund's name and its local and paused flags from the fund registry.

A scraper fetches its fund's site and returns every company as a name, a category and a link. Sources vary with how the site is built: server-rendered HTML, a Next.js payload, a WordPress or CMS API, Webflow paging, or a detail page per company fetched in batches. The category is a comma-joined list of what the page says about the company (sectors, stage, place, year), and a company the fund is out of gets the Exited tag. When a redesign renames companies, a STORED_AS map keeps them under their stored names so they are not taken for new ones.

A scraper throws when the page's markup moves, so a broken page is reported rather than read as an empty portfolio. The scrape controller runs the scrapers; nothing calls them directly.
